CMS Medical Necessity and Unit Limits (simplified excerpt)

Billed units must reflect the medically reasonable and necessary quantity
for the procedure performed. Units billed far in excess of standard clinical
guidelines require additional documentation and may be denied as not
medically necessary. The procedure code billed must also be clinically
consistent with the patient's age, sex, and diagnosis; implausible
combinations of procedure code, diagnosis, and patient demographics must be
flagged for review before payment. For example, billing twenty or more
units of a joint injection procedure that is typically performed once
exceeds reasonable and necessary limits. Likewise, billing an obstetric
delivery procedure for a pediatric patient is not clinically plausible.
