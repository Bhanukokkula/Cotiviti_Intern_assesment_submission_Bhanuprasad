CMS Program Integrity Manual (simplified excerpt) - Coding Accuracy and Upcoding

Providers must bill the CPT or HCPCS code that most accurately reflects the
level of service actually performed and documented. Selecting a
higher-complexity evaluation and management code than is supported by the
patient's diagnosis and clinical documentation, known as upcoding, is
prohibited. Claims suspected of upcoding must be flagged for review and may
result in claim denial, recoupment, and provider audit. For example, billing
a high-complexity office visit code when the diagnosis reflects only a
routine, low-complexity examination is a common upcoding pattern.
