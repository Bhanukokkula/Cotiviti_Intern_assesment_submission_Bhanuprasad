CMS Claims Processing Manual (simplified excerpt) - Duplicate Claims

A claim is considered a duplicate if it matches a previously submitted claim
on provider identifier, patient identifiers, procedure code, service date,
and billed amount. Duplicate claims must be denied or flagged for provider
follow-up. Only one payment is permitted per unique service rendered.
Providers who repeatedly submit duplicate claims may be referred for audit.
For example, submitting the same office visit for the same patient and
provider on the same date and for the same billed amount a second time is a
duplicate submission and must be flagged.
